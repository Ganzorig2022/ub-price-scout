4 · Before you go

Café / office: bring only the mini's figure‑8 power cable. The power supply is inside — no charger box.
FileVault: after a shutdown the password screen may not see a Bluetooth keyboard. Carry a USB‑C cable for a wired keyboard.
No battery: pulling the cable = a power cut. Shut down first.
Weight: mini + monitor + keys + bank ≈ 2.4 kg. A MacBook Air is 1.24 kg.
